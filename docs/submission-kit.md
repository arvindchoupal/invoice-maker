## Primary launch story

### Headline

> InvoiceWala launches a free utility suite for invoices, GST and payment follow-ups

### Launch summary

> Small businesses often need a quick invoice, GST calculation, HSN/SAC code or payment reminder—not a heavy accounting setup. InvoiceWala brings these jobs into one browser-based toolkit. Its free public suite includes 14 tools, led by invoice creation, GST utilities, document generators and a transparent HSN/SAC Finder.

### Why it was built

> Many freelancers and small businesses still move between Word templates, spreadsheets, calculators and copied WhatsApp messages. InvoiceWala is being built to connect those scattered tasks into a simple workflow: calculate, create the document, send it and track the payment.

### Differentiation

- Utility-first: users can complete useful tasks before creating an account.
- Connected workflow: quotation → invoice → receipt → payment reminder.
- India GST depth without making the entire platform India-only.
- Transparent HSN/SAC methodology and official verification links.
- Lightweight alternative for users who do not need a full accounting suite.

## Standard answers

### Is InvoiceWala free?

Yes. Public tools and a free account are available. Pro is currently ₹199/month during early access in India.

### Does it replace an accountant?

No. InvoiceWala helps create and organize billing records. Tax classification, filing and compliance decisions should be verified with official sources or a qualified professional.

### Is it only for India?

No. Core invoice and business-document workflows are designed for global English-speaking users. India-specific GST tools are an additional specialization.

### Does it require installation?

No. InvoiceWala is browser-based.

### What makes it different?

Users can complete practical public-tool tasks before signup, then connect those results to saved invoice, client and payment workflows.
