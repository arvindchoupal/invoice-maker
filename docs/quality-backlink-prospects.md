# InvoiceWala Quality Backlink Prospect List

Research date: 23 June 2026  
Goal: 20 quality referring domains without paid-link farms, PBNs, comment spam or bulk guest-post networks.

## Quality standard

A prospect qualifies only when it has:

- Strong relevance to accounting, GST, freelancing, SaaS or small business.
- A real editorial team, active community or curated submission process.
- Indexed pages with original content.
- A natural reason to mention a useful InvoiceWala asset.
- No requirement to buy a followed backlink.

Directory links may be `nofollow`. They are separated from editorial opportunities because their main value is entity discovery, referral traffic and a natural link profile.

## Assets required before outreach

Do not pitch the homepage alone. Lead with one of these:

1. HSN/SAC Code Finder — expand beyond the current starter dataset and document the source/update method.
2. Embeddable GST Calculator — free widget with optional attribution.
3. Payment Reminder Message Library — data-backed collection by delay, tone and channel.
4. GST Invoice Checklist — downloadable one-page reference with official source links.
5. Freelancer Invoice Kit — invoice, estimate, receipt and reminder templates in one ungated pack.

## Recommended campaign sequence

### Week 1: make the asset link-worthy

- Expand HSN/SAC Finder coverage and publish the update methodology.
- Build a standalone GST Invoice Checklist PDF/HTML asset.
- Add an embed option to the GST calculator.
- Create a press/resource page with logo, screenshots, founder bio and product facts.

### Week 2: secure foundational citations

Submit to:

1. AlternativeTo
2. BetaList
3. Startup Stash
4. Indie Hackers
5. Uneed
6. F6S

Prepare Product Hunt but launch only after the utility suite is polished.

### Weeks 3–6: editorial outreach

- Send five highly personalized pitches per week.
- Start with CAclubindia, TaxGuru, Millo, Freelancers Union and AccountingWEB.
- Pitch one specific asset and explain exactly which existing article or audience need it serves.
- Do not attach files. Link to a public, fast-loading resource.

### Weeks 4–10: partner outreach

- Prospect five CA firms, five freelancer communities and five agencies per week.
- Offer a useful embed, checklist or co-branded resource.
- Ask for feedback first; the link should be a natural consequence of using the asset.

## Quality-control checklist

Reject any opportunity that:

- Sells a “permanent dofollow backlink” package.
- Publishes unrelated gambling, casino, loans, crypto and health articles together.
- Has no visible editorial standards or real audience.
- Requires reciprocal linking.
- Uses sitewide footer/sidebar links.
- Promises a specific Domain Authority increase.
- Wants spun or generic AI guest posts.
- Has pages disappearing from Google or obviously copied content.

Before accepting a link:

- Confirm the linking page is indexable.
- Confirm the article is topically relevant.
- Prefer a contextual link inside original content.
- Use branded or descriptive natural anchor text.
- Avoid repeating exact-match anchors such as “free invoice generator” across many sites.
- Track the publication URL, anchor, target page and date.

## Realistic outcome

The first campaign should target:

- 6–8 curated platform citations.
- 6–10 editorial/resource links.
- 6–10 accountant, community or agency partner links.

This requires approximately 80–120 carefully selected contacts. A realistic acquisition period is 8–16 weeks. Placement is never guaranteed; anyone guaranteeing 20 editorial links quickly is usually selling low-quality links.
